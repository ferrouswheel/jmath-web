## Description

Dividing the unit-circle identity by the squared horizontal coordinate relates tangent to secant. It also explains why tangent becomes unbounded near a vertical radius.

## Conditions

cos(θ) ≠ 0; sec(θ) means 1/cos(θ).

## Statement

$$
1+\tan^2\theta = \frac{1}{\cos^2\theta}.
$$

## History

Tangent and secant developed alongside the older chord and sine methods of trigonometry.

## Worked example

At θ = π/4, 1 + 1 = 1/(1/2) = 2.

## Proof sketch

Divide sin² θ + cos² θ = 1 by cos² θ. Division is valid only away from the tangent poles.
