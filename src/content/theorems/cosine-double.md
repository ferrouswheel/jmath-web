## Description

Doubling an angle turns cosine into a quadratic expression in either sine or cosine. The two forms are useful when only one of those functions is known.

## Conditions

Any real angle θ.

## Statement

$$
\cos(2\theta) = 2\cos^2\theta-1 = 1-2\sin^2\theta.
$$

## History

Double-angle relations belong to the angle-combination techniques used in constructing trigonometric tables.

## Worked example

At θ = π/4, cos(π/2) = 2(1/2) − 1 = 0.

## Proof sketch

Set α = β = θ in the cosine addition formula, then use sin² θ + cos² θ = 1.
