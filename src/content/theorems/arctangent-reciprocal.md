## Description

Positive reciprocal slopes correspond to the two acute angles of one right triangle. Their principal arctangents therefore add to a right angle.

## Conditions

Real x > 0.

## Statement

$$
\arctan x+\arctan\!\left(\frac1x\right) = \frac\pi2.
$$

## History

Complementary-angle relationships are part of the triangle-based tradition from which modern trigonometric functions developed.

## Worked example

arctan(1) + arctan(1) = π/4 + π/4.

## Proof sketch

In a right triangle with positive legs 1 and x, the two acute angles have tangents x and 1/x and sum to π/2. For x < 0, the corresponding sum is −π/2.
